03 / ARCHITECTURE & SAFETY

Local first.
Remote by consent.

Studio can work with portable files without account access. The handheld remains usable offline and retains authority over applying settings.

01 / PORTABLE

Profiles are data.

Declarative JSON describes layouts, ordering, labels, supported settings and references to installed contributions.

02 / VALIDATED

Schema before apply.

The local engine validates and migrates a profile, stages changes, backs up the active selection and retains rollback.

03 / TRUSTED

Code needs governance.

Executable modules require signatures, review, capabilities, sandboxing, revocation and controlled activation.

04 / OPTIONAL

Cloud is additive.

Pairing and sync are opt-in future services. No inbound device ports or exposed host-control endpoints are required.
